## About Artiveda

**Artiveda is a technology company focused on building useful, thoughtful, and scalable digital products.**

Our approach brings together technology, creativity, and problem-solving to explore solutions for real-world needs.

We believe meaningful technology should be practical, accessible, and designed with people in mind.

## Our Vision

To create technology that enables progress, opens new possibilities, and makes a lasting impact.

## What We Explore

- **Digital Products** — Web and mobile applications designed around real needs.
- **Business Solutions** — Digital tools that support business operations and workflows.
- **Education Technology** — Technology-led approaches to school operations and student development.
- **Digital Experiences** — Thoughtful interfaces and accessible user experiences.

### Nexora
**School Management Platform · Product Concept**

Nexora is the working name for Artiveda's first product direction: a school management platform being shaped around the needs of small and growing private schools.

**Areas being explored**

- School and teacher management
- Admissions and student records
- Attendance and timetable management
- Examinations, report cards, and fee records
- School calendars and activity planning
- Student exploration and development

**Product direction:** Hindi-first · Practical workflows · Low-bandwidth awareness

<sub>Status: Product concept and planning. The working name may change; the listed areas describe the product direction, not a claim that all features are currently released.</sub>

## Contributions & Collaboration

We value thoughtful collaboration and practical ideas.

If a public Artiveda repository is open for contributions:

1. Review its README and contribution instructions.
2. Check open issues before starting work.
3. Discuss substantial changes before submitting a pull request.
4. Keep contributions focused, clear, and well documented.
